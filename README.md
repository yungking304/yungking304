## Operations work I'm proud of

- **Paper → digital.** Led the plant's transition to digital inspection records (~1,500 records/day) and standardized QA documentation across departments.
- **Audit record.** Every third-party audit at 90%+ and SQF/BRC "A+" grades since 2012; USDA non-compliances down year over year for five straight years with zero record-keeping NRs.
- **Built from scratch:** a pre-op RODAC microbial monitoring program, a cross-plant QA operator training system with digital checklists, and an FY tracking workbook driven by Excel/VBA + Python.
